What it counts
A view each time someone opens the homepage, a project page or this page. Reloading or returning to the same page within eight hours is not counted again.
Not people
One visitor who opens several pages records several views, so this is not a count of people.
How fresh
The public total updates at least once a day, usually every few hours, so the newest views can take a while to appear.
Not counted
Bots, local test copies of the site, and browsers that block analytics.
Privacy
Counted with GoatCounter, privacy-friendly analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, keeps no IP addresses or visitor IDs, and records only anonymous totals such as page, day, country and browser.
